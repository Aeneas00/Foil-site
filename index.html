Why it exists

People plan years and forget them. Foiled turns a year into one object you can hold, keep, or hand to someone you love.

The name is a memory stamped in foil, the way a trading card gets its metallic finish. The card is a memory. It carries a stamp, the year, a motto you wrote, and a line such as “Held 212 days”.
How it works

Four steps from plan to card

  1. 01

    Plan

    Write one yearly goal and split it into 3 to 8 milestones, each with a date.

  2. 02

    Design

    Pick the colour, the picture, the motto and the effect before the year starts. The card is a promise.

  3. 03

    Seal

    Sealing locks the goal, the dates, the milestones and the design. You get one date change per goal.

  4. 04

    Earn

    Finish every milestone and the card is yours. Miss any and it is still issued, torn in two, as a permanent record.

The rules

Six rules give a card its meaning

1

Design first, earn second

You pick the look before the year starts, so the card is a promise.

2

Sealed means locked

The goal cannot be edited later. One date change is allowed per goal.

3

Rarity comes from effort

More milestones over a longer span give a higher tier.

4

Late costs a tier

A milestone finished after its date lowers an earned card by one tier. The band then reads “weathered”.

5

Failure still leaves a card

An unfinished goal produces a torn card with a grey, faded finish. It is kept and never deleted.

6

Cards can be gifted

Hand one to a friend. Cards are never sold and carry no money value.

When a year falls short

Failure still leaves a card

Finish 3 of 5 milestones and the card is issued torn in two, with a grey finish and the stamp “3/5 · Unfinished”. It stays in your collection, kept and never deleted.

The torn card is a record of the year you had. It looks dignified, and nothing in the app humiliates you for it.

Finish a milestone after its date and an earned card drops one tier. Its band reads “weathered”.

Collection

Every year you finish goes on the shelf

Earned, sealed and torn cards live in one collection, each with the days you held it. A runner’s Rare crimson card sits beside a degree in Legendary gold.

A card can be gifted to a friend. It is never sold, and it never carries a price.
