How it works

One loop: plan, work, earn, roll.

Plan and commit

Add a task for a day. Commit to it before midnight the day before to earn full coins.

Start and finish

Press Start on the day. Done unlocks after half of your time estimate has passed.

Earn coins

High priority pays 30, Medium 20, Low 10. 50 coins buy one roll.

Roll a card

Each roll gives a card with a design, a tier and a serial number, such as Crown #47 of 200.

Cards you own can be applied to your open tasks. The task gets the card's frame and icon.

Fair play

Coins, cards and serial numbers are written only by the server. The app on your phone cannot change them.

  • Work has a minimum timeYou cannot finish a task seconds after starting it.
  • One task at a timeOnly one task can be in progress, so you cannot start five and wait.
  • Commitments are realA task counts as committed only if you committed it before the planned day began.
  • Finished tasks are lockedYou cannot edit or delete a finished task to reset the daily cap or the repeat filter.
  • Time zone is fixedIt can be changed once every 7 days, so the day boundary cannot be moved to get a second cap.
  • Cards are yoursYou can only apply cards that are in your own collection.
