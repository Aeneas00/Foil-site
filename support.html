Support

Foiled is in development. If you have a question, found a bug or want your data removed, write to us.

Emailfoiled.app@gmail.com Instagram@foiled.app

Questions

What is Foiled?

Foiled makes digital collectible cards, like the physical kind. You earn each card by finishing a goal you set yourself.

How does rarity work?

Rarity is partly random. The longer you make the goal, the higher it can climb, from Common to Rare, Epic and Legendary.

Can I change a card after I seal it?

No. You design the card, then seal it. It stays sealed until you are ready to unpack it. From that moment it is yours, and nothing on it can be changed.

Who can I trade with?

Friends, family or anyone else in the world, as long as you have at least one card to trade.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us from the address on your account and ask for deletion. Our privacy policy explains what we delete.
